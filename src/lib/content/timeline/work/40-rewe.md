**Role:** Building a self-service integration platform on Kubernetes, so development teams get what they need without tickets or waiting on anyone, as part of the company's digitalization and its merger with its international counterparts. Today it is the shared foundation for REWE, used by development teams across multiple European countries.

<!-- more -->

- Providing Kafka as a platform service: operating, maintaining and backing it up, and writing the self-service operator around it.
- Providing the Istio service mesh as a platform service, with its own self-service operator so teams can onboard themselves.
- Providing API cataloging with Gravitee as a platform service, where teams register and manage their APIs through self-service operators.
- Cataloging the schemas behind those APIs and events, and giving their consumers, both automated systems and human teams, ways to discover, view and test them.
- Designing and building multi-cluster Kubernetes operators in Go that isolate tenants into their own clusters to keep the blast radius small, while managing all of them from one central place.
- Creating a shared operator library, so new operators can be built faster on common foundations for multi-cluster handling, configuration, validation and testing.
- Building supporting APIs and tooling in Go, including an automated local Kubernetes environment that brings up the full end-to-end setup of the platform on a developer machine.
- Shaping the next-generation architecture for the integration tools across the merged organization.
- Working closely with sister platform teams to grow a shared self-service platform.
